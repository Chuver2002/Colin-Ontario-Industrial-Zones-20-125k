If you lease space here

  • Start 12–18 months before expiration. For 20k–125k SF, the best options often trade before they hit the open market.
  • Rents are down about 10% from a year ago, so renewals are a negotiation, not a formality. Benchmark your current rate before your landlord sends terms.
  • Concessions are back: free rent, improvement allowances and flexible terms are on the table, especially in buildings that have sat vacant.
  • Price a relocation even if you plan to stay. A credible alternative is the strongest renewal leverage you have.

If you own a building here

  • Vacancy in IE West is falling (6.1% → 4.8% this year per Lee & Associates), so well-located 20k–125k SF buildings are leasing again.
  • Sale pricing has softened to about $246/SF on average, while recent Ontario trades show strong buyers for quality and yard-heavy (IOS) sites.
  • Renewing a tenant early usually beats downtime plus free rent. Know your tenant's expiration and talk 12+ months out.
  • New supply is picking up (5.8M SF under construction), so positioning matters: power, yard, clear height and loading set the rent.
